# Privacy policy

*Last updated: 26 November 2026*

Deal Radar Ireland ("Deal Radar", "we") runs the website dealradar.ie and the Deal Radar app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. Lei Zhang, 69 Mespil Road, Dublin 4, Ireland is responsible for your data. Contact: info@dealradar.ie.

## Without an account
You can browse every deal without signing in. We don't ask for your name, email or location.
- **Clicks on deals.** When you tap a deal we record which deal was tapped, so we know which deals are useful. Without an account this isn't linked to you.
- **Notifications.** If you allow notifications in the app, we store your phone's notification token and which notifications you chose. We use it only to send those notifications.
- **On this device.** The website remembers which deals you have already seen, in your browser only, so it can mark new ones.

## With an account
When you sign in with Apple, Google or an email link, we keep:
- your **email address** (with Sign in with Apple this can be a private relay address) and an account number;
- what you **follow**: networks and shops, search words, and links to products you watch with your target price or discount;
- the **deals you tap**, so you can see your activity and we can improve the service;
- your **membership level**.

We use this only to run the service: your own deal list, searches for your words, price checks for products you watch, and the notifications you chose.

## Who processes your data
- **Google Firebase and Google Cloud** (sign-in, database, hosting, notifications), in the EU (europe-west1) where possible.
- **Apple** delivers notifications to iPhones; **Sign in with Apple** if you use it.
We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

## Shops and affiliate links
Tapping a deal opens the shop's own website or app, which has its own privacy policy. Some Amazon links are affiliate links (Amazon Associates): Amazon may know you came from Deal Radar, and we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

## How long we keep it
Account data is kept while you have an account. Records of taps are kept for as long as you have an account. Notification tokens are removed when the app is deleted or the token stops working. Deal prices are not personal data.

## Your choices and rights
- Turn notifications on or off at any time in the app's Settings or in iPhone Settings.
- **Delete your account** in the app (Settings > Delete account) or by emailing us. This removes your account and everything linked to it.
- Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. You can also complain to the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

## Children
Deal Radar is not aimed at children under 16.

## Changes
If this policy changes we will update the date above, and tell members by email or in the app if the change is significant.

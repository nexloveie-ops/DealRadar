# Deal Radar support

Need help with the Deal Radar app or dealradar.ie? Email us at info@dealradar.ie and we'll reply within 2 working days.

## Common questions

**How often are prices checked?**
Twice a day, around 7am and 7pm Irish time. Each deal says when its price was last checked. The shop's own page always has the final price.

**What does the old price mean?**
For phone networks, the same phone's price when you don't switch or without a loyalty card. For most shops, the shop's own Was price. Powercity doesn't show one, so we use a higher price we saw there ourselves. For Amazon, the RRP or Was price on Amazon.

**Why didn't I get a notification?**
Check that notifications are on for Deal Radar in iPhone Settings and in the app's Settings. We send at most one notification per price check, none between 10pm and 7am, and only when there's something new.

**How do I watch a product?**
Sign in, open Settings, tap Watch a product and paste a product link from Amazon.ie, Harvey Norman, Expert, Powercity or Mobile123. Choose any discount, a discount of at least X%, or a price below €X.

**How do I delete my account?**
In the app: Settings > Delete account. On the website, or if you can't sign in, email us from the address you signed up with.

**Is Deal Radar run by the shops?**
No. Deal Radar is independent. Some Amazon links are affiliate links: if you buy, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

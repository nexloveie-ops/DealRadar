How prices are checked

We read prices from each shop's own website twice a day, at 7am and 7pm. Every deal shows when its price was checked. Prices can change after that, so the shop's page has the final price.

What the old price means

For operators it is the same phone's price when you don't switch, or without Clubcard. For Expert and Powercity it is the shop's own Was price. For Amazon it is the RRP or Was price on Amazon. Where Amazon doesn't show us a price, the price comes from a deals site and says so.
